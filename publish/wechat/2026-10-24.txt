📰 02｜论文 · The Journal of Finance：期权隐含信念的波动是否过大？

引文：Augenblick & Lazarus (2026). Excess Movement in Option‐Implied Beliefs. The Journal of Finance.
主页： https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.70086 

📰 03｜论文 · The Review of Financial Studies：公共养老金为什么转向另类资产？

引文：Begenau, Liang & Siriwardane (2026). The Rise of Alternatives. The Review of Financial Studies.
